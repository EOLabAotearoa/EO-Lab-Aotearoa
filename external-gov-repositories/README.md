# <p align="center">External Government & Research Repositories </p>


This folder contains a curated list of links to external GitHub repositories from New Zealand government agencies and Crown Research Institutes.  
We link directly to their official repos so users always get the most up-to-date code and resources.

---

## <p align="center">Government Agencies </p>

- **Department of Conservation (DOC)** – [GitHub Pages](https://docgovtnz.github.io/)  
  Tools and resources supporting conservation projects across Aotearoa.  

- **Land Information New Zealand (LINZ)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/linz)  
  Geospatial datasets, mapping tools, and open data services.  

- **Ministry for Primary Industries (MPI)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/Ministry-for-Primary-Industries)  
  Data and tools for agriculture, fisheries, and biosecurity.  

- **Ministry for the Environment (MfE)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/mfe-nz)  
  Environmental data and reporting tools.  

- **Stats NZ** – [GitHub](https://github.com/statisticsnz)  
  Open statistics, APIs, and data resources.  

- **NZ Transport Agency (NZTA / Waka Kotahi)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/NZTA)  
  Transport and infrastructure-related data and applications.  

---

## <p align="center">Crown Research Institutes </p>

- **AgResearch** – [GitHub](https://github.com/AgResearch)  
  Agricultural science and land-use research tools.  

- **ESR (Institute of Environmental Science & Research)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/ESR-NZ)  
  Data and methods for health, environment, and forensic science.  

- **GNS Science** – [GitHub](https://github.com/GNS-Science)  
  Earth science, geology, and hazard research resources.  

- **Manaaki Whenua – Landcare Research** – [GitHub](https://github.com/manaakiwhenua)  
  Biodiversity, ecosystems, and land resource science tools.  

- **NIWA (National Institute of Water & Atmospheric Research)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/niwa)  
  Climate, freshwater, and marine science resources.  

- **Scion (Forest Research Institute)** – [GitHub](https://github.com/ScionResearch)  
  Forestry and biomaterials research.  

- **Plant & Food Research** – [GitHub](https://github.com/PlantandFoodResearch)  
  Tools supporting horticulture, agriculture, and food science.
